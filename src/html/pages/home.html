About

Hello! Thanks for stopping by. I am an engineer working on computational sciences in the area of advanced finite element methods, fracture and fatigue behavior of materials, structural stability, machine learning methods and computational fluid dynamics.

Research interests

Areas I work in and want to keep building on.

Finite element methods

Corotational beam and truss, flexibility-based fiber and constant strain triangle formulations.

Fatigue & damage mechanics

Continuum damage models with cycle-jump algorithms for ductile materials, correlated with test data.

Structural stability & dynamics

Flutter and dynamic instability of functionally graded shells under nonconservative follower loads.

Computational fluid dynamics

Inviscid vortex-method solvers (LVM, UVLM) for nonlinear fluid–structure interaction.

Multiscale modeling

Linking material behaviour at smaller scales to the structural response of components.

Machine learning

Data-driven and surrogate approaches to accelerate and augment mechanics simulations.
